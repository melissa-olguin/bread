About Us

Story of our bakery

Hi! We are North Star Bakery, a family-owned business dedicated to providing the highest quality baked goods with a personal touch.

At North Star Bakery, we believe in using only the finest ingredients, sourced locally whenever possible. Our team of skilled bakers is passionate about creating delicious treats that bring joy to our customers.

We are committed to sustainability and community engagement, supporting local farmers and participating in various charitable initiatives. Our mission is to create a warm and welcoming environment where everyone can enjoy the simple pleasure of freshly baked goods.

Thank you for choosing North Star Bakery. We look forward to serving you and sharing our love for baking with you!

Sourcing Philosophy

Our sourcing philosophy is centered around supporting local farmers and producers. We believe in building strong relationships with our suppliers and ensuring that our ingredients are of the highest quality and freshness.

Staff Highlights

Our team of skilled bakers brings years of experience and a passion for their craft. Each member of our staff is dedicated to creating exceptional baked goods that exceed our customers' expectations.
